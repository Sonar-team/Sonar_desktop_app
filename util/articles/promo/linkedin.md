# LinkedIn

## Option A — Short post (recommended, ~120 words)

Some networks can't be scanned.

Industrial control systems where an active scan can trip a PLC. Air-gapped enclaves with no route to the internet. OT segments where "just run nmap" ends careers.

We built **Sonar** for exactly those networks.

It's an open-source (Rust + Tauri) desktop app that listens — passively, offline — and turns raw traffic into a trustworthy flow matrix and graph. It emits nothing on the network it observes, speaks industrial protocols (Modbus, PROFINET, OPC UA, S7Comm, EtherNet/IP), and every release is reproducible-built and signed so you can prove the binary wasn't tampered with.

It won't touch your network. It'll just tell you the truth about it.

100 releases, 2,300+ downloads, and free under AGPLv3 👇

## Tips
- First 2 lines are all most people see in the feed — lead with the hook, not the product name.
- Put the GitHub link on its own line near the end; links mid-post reduce reach.
- Post Tue–Thu morning (your audience's timezone) for best B2B reach.
- Reply to your own post with a second comment adding a screenshot — boosts dwell time.
